About Us

AgroCare

AgroCare is a Peruvian agricultural technology startup founded by a team of Software Engineering students from the Peruvian University of Applied Sciences (UPC). It was created in response to a real and urgent problem in the Peruvian livestock sector: the lack of accessible digital tools that allow producers, both independent and commercial, to manage their livestock efficiently, traceably, and sustainably.

Ranchers

Bovix is an intuitive mobile platform that gives you control over your livestock operations efficiently and in an organized manner. With our app, you can register and monitor in real-time the health, feeding, and reproduction of each animal, receive automatic alerts, and access veterinary contacts, working even in rural areas with low connectivity. All in one place, so you can focus on making informed decisions and improving the productivity and well-being of your livestock without complications.

Veterinarians

Bovix is an integrated digital tool that facilitates the management of your clinical work both in the office and in the field. With our platform, you can access and centralize clinical histories, streamline the registration of diagnoses, and keep a structured control of treatments and reproductive tracking. It optimizes appointment coordination and direct communication with farmers, allowing you to provide a more efficient, precise, and organized veterinary medical service.

For ranchers
